How the Custom Plate Test works

  • You will be shown 1,000 license plates one at a time
  • Only the fields you checked above appear as inputs — type each one
  • Press Tab ↹ to move between fields, Enter ↵ to submit
  • The test is timed — your speed & accuracy are measured
  • Results are sent to the "Custom Field Test" tab in the sheet

How the Ambiguous Letters test works

  • You will be shown plate numbers only — no state, prefix or suffix
  • Every plate hides one visually tricky character (O/D, X/K, M/W, Y/V, etc.)
  • The plate is rendered squished, blurred & glared — just like a rushed real-world glance
  • Look closely, type the exact code, then press Enter ↵
  • Results are sent to the "Ambiguous Letters Test" tab in the sheet

How the Texas Vanity test works

  • You will be shown 100 Texas plates; Texas prints the letter O and digit 0 identically
  • Beside another number → digit 0 (JACK01, DDS01)
  • Standard layout ABC-1234 / AB1-C234 → digit 0 (Texas never issues the letter O)
  • Reads as a word, or no numbers beside it → letter O (ROVEHER, G4RMO)
  • Skip separators (★ ♥ • -), type the registration, press Enter ↵. Missed plates are explained at the end
